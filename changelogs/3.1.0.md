**Mentra App and glasses**

- Improved glasses pairing and reconnection, including on iPad.
- Improved Android background reliability for captions and miniapps.
- Added Xingyi AR99 support.
- More reliable photo and video capture, with improved shutter sounds and privacy-light timing. Taking photos no longer interrupts your running miniapp.
- Added video thumbnails and smoother gallery browsing.
- Easier Mentra Live updates through your phone, with clearer progress and better recovery from interruptions.

- **Mentra AI:** Control whether photos are included with questions. Improved visual answers and connected-app follow-ups.

- **Translation:** Restored all 60 languages.
- **Mentra Maps:** Clearer directions on G1/G2, less display flickering, and improved Android offline routing.
- **Livestreamer:** More video-quality controls and custom streaming options, plus improved startup and sharing.
